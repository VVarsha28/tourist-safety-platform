Tourist Safety Dos & Don'ts

Recommended Dos
  • Keep your Digital Tourist ID or paper pass with you at all times.
  • Always use pre-paid taxi stands or verified ride-hailing apps.
  • Stick to illuminated primary corridors in markets after 8 PM.
  • Drink only sealed bottled water from reputable vendors.
Vital Don'ts & Scams
  • Never purchase precious gems from unofficial street runners.
  • Decline offers from "friendly guides" saying monuments are closed.
  • Avoid entering isolated mountain trails after dusk without a permit.
  • Do not leave mobile phones in open rear trouser pockets.

🚨 Immediate Action Checklist for Emergencies:

1 Trigger SafeTrail SOS: Hit the floating red button to transmit your GPS coordinates and medical vitals snapshot.
2 Seek Public Shelter: Enter any registered hotel lobby, bank ATM vestibule, or official police assistance booth.
3 Call 112: Quote your Digital ID code (e.g. {{ current_user.profile.digital_id_code }}) for priority incident dispatch.
